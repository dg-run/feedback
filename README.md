# dg.run Feedback

Welcome to the dg.run community feedback hub!

This repository is the place to share your ideas, report bugs, ask questions, and help shape the future of **dg.run**.

## What can you do here?

- 🐛 **Report bugs:** Let us know when something isn't working as expected.
- 💡 **Request features:** Suggest new features and improvements.
- 💬 **Share feedback:** Tell us what you like, what you don't, and what could be better.
- ❓ **Ask questions:** Get help and discuss dg.run with the community.

## How to contribute

Before submitting feedback:

1. Search existing issues and discussions to avoid duplicates.
2. For bugs, open a new issue and include steps to reproduce the problem.
3. For feature requests and general feedback, start a discussion.
4. Be respectful and provide as much detail as possible.

## Community guidelines

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Keep discussions relevant to dg.run.
- Explain problems clearly and provide examples when possible.
- Don't share passwords, API keys, personal information, or other sensitive data.

## About dg.run

dg.run is a platform for building and running digital products, from AI agents and applications to websites and more.

Your feedback helps us build a better platform.
